## Gotchas / watch-outs
- **Scaling the watermark up moves the Start figure's head *further* under the Direction
  box, not clear of it.** The figure sits in the maze's bottom row and grows upward from
  its cell centre. The owner wanted it bigger *and* the head/body visible with only the
  legs cut, which are opposing moves — so the panel is pushed **down** as it is scaled up.
  Current numbers put the head 34pt clear of the box. The arithmetic is spelled out in
  `CoverPage.tsx`'s `WATERMARK` comment.
- **The watermark's overflow must stay clipped** (`overflow: hidden` on the body-band
  wrapper). Unclipped overflow on a fixed-height print page can spill into an extra blank
  PDF page. Verified none appears — the check is in the verify command below.
- **Closing the pill → instruction gap alone opens a worse hole in the middle of the box.**
  Both gaps have to move together.
- **The watermark's badge cannot sit directly above its maze.** At that scale the position
  falls inside the opaque title band. It lives in the ~59pt strip between the band and the
  Direction box, aligned to the body's visible left edge rather than the maze's clipped
  one.
- Still true from the previous handoff, and still the easiest way to waste an hour:
  **`npx tsc --noEmit` in `Web App/frontend` typechecks ZERO files.** Use `npm run build`.
- Still true: **don't "simplify" the `@font-face` block** in `src/index.css` — the
  `Roboto-Bold` family exists because the designer's SVG names that family with no
  `font-weight`.
- **Still stale, deliberately:** `LevelDashboardPage.tsx` renders row 0 as a locked
  "Cover / Tutorial" card and `levelStore.startNewLevel` reserves `pages[0]`, but the cover

- Playwright's `waitUntil: 'networkidle'` intermittently timed out against the Vite dev

  reliable combination.
